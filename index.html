Research

Behavioral & experimental finance

My research combines large-scale investor transaction data with online and laboratory experiments to understand how past experience affects beliefs, attention, memory, and trading behavior.

My current work spans investor memory and trading decisions, associative portfolio representations, and the effects of return frequency and framing on investment choice.

Research interests

01

Investor behavior

How investors' own histories and experiences shape selling, repurchasing, and portfolio decisions.

02

Memory & associations

How financial experiences are remembered and how assets become mentally associated within portfolios.

03

Decision environments

How the frequency, horizon, and presentation of returns affect risk taking and financial choice.

Methods & tools

Data + experiments

I work with administrative and brokerage transaction data as well as experimental data. My research workflow includes Stata, Python, MATLAB, SQL, and oTree.
