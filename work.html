Research

Research

I study investor behavior using large-scale transaction data and experiments, with a focus on experience, memory, attention, and the way financial information is represented.

Current research

Investor memory, selling & repurchasing

Research on how investors' past experiences with an asset shape subsequent selling and repurchasing decisions. The project combines detailed investor transaction histories with survival-analysis methods.

Current research

Associative portfolio representations

Research on whether assets that are experienced together become associated in investors' mental representations of their portfolios, and whether those associations predict future trading decisions.

Experimental finance

Return frequency & investment horizon

Experimental work studying how the frequency with which losses are experienced, together with the investment horizon, affects portfolio choice and risk taking.

Experimental finance

Financial information & choice

A broader stream of experimental work on how the presentation and complexity of financial information influence beliefs and investment decisions.

Materials

Code & replication materials

Selected code and research materials will be made available as projects become ready for public release. Restricted investor data are not publicly distributable.
